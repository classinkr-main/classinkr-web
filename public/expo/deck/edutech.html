Case studies

ClassIn in schools and higher education.

Real classrooms, from Singapore to Riyadh.

Secondary school · Singapore

Hwa Chong Institution Senior School

Keeping Teachers at the Centre of an AI-Enabled Classroom

The challenge
Teachers need their materials on screen while they work through ideas live, without another complicated workflow.
With ClassIn
Course materials stay on one screen while the teacher writes live on the other, with AI tools for maths, science and Chinese.
The difference
Teachers move between prepared content and live explanation without switching screens; the AI supports their judgement, not replaces it.

Higher Education · Singapore

Temasek Polytechnic School of Business

Making Project-Based Learning Visible

The challenge
Group work was spread across tables and laptops, so sharing an idea meant photographing a whiteboard and interrupting the discussion.
With ClassIn
Two flexible learning spaces where interactive displays and simple screen sharing put every group’s work in a common view.
The difference
In a Digital Banking lesson, groups move from discussion to presentation and feedback within the same activity.

Higher Education · Singapore

National University of Singapore Centre for Teaching & Learning (CTLT)

Making Every Voice Heard in the Seminar Room

The challenge
Wireless microphones slow a seminar down: they have to be worn, passed between speakers and kept charged.
With ClassIn
Ceiling microphones and in-room speakers capture every voice, with nothing to wear or hold.
The difference
The lesson starts without handing out microphones, and the focus stays on the conversation, not the equipment.

K–12 International · Riyadh · Bangkok

EtonHouse International Schools New Campus Openings

Ready for Teaching from Day One

The challenge
A new campus needs every classroom ready for real lessons on opening day, and teachers trained before they start.
With ClassIn
A standard classroom package of boards, cameras and ceiling microphones, with delivery, installation and teacher training.
The difference
Every classroom has the same setup before students arrive, so teaching starts from day one.
